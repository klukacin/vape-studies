# Wiip research update — 21 September 2026

Scope: Wiip Magnetic II and Wiip X Pro, Croatian product offering. Sources #143–151 in `app/src/data/studies.js` contain original URLs, editorial adaptations and limitations. Prices are snapshots, not ongoing price feeds.

## Evidence and gaps

- Manufacturer product pages: Magnetic II 400 mAh / 3.7 V, €14.99; X Pro Pink €9.99 and charging up to 30 min. No watts, coil material/resistance or measured operating temperatures found in these specifications.
- Manufacturer pod declarations: Magnetic Cola 10 mg/mL, 2 mL, 0.07 mg/puff; Cola 18 mg/mL, 2 mL, 0.14 mg/puff; Apple 10 mg/mL, 2 mL, 0.05 mg/puff. Each €4.20. No public puff protocol, device/mode specification or uncertainty on those pages. Do not interpret these as systemic uptake or generalize to all flavors/modes.
- Manufacturer conformity page names AFNOR, EMC, RoHS and drop-testing standards, but does not attach reports or model-specific emission graphs.
- Web queries included Wiip + emissions/carbonyl/clinical/test report, exact model names and PubMed-restricted search. Europe PMC REST `TITLE_ABS:wiip` returned hitCount 0. This is a limited search, not proof that no studies exist.
- Montenegro original register: full 27-page PDF copied unchanged. Relevant pages 17 and 26–27 concern older Wiip products/documents (2018–2020), not confirmed Magnetic II / X Pro tests. AVERlabC180035 is referenced but the report itself is not included or retrieved.
- Spain's notification register has a Magnetic II entry but its 49 MB whole-register PDF exceeds the hosting asset size limit; not mirrored or presented as a study. Malta PDF download attempts failed; no local copy or claim of one.
